At 43, with five losses in his last six fights, Stephen "Wonderboy" Thompson walked into Madison Square Garden for the last fight of his career. Two minutes and fifty-four seconds into the third round of UFC 334, he landed the same spinning wheel kick that knocked out Jake Ellenberger eleven years earlier, put Charles Radtke down, laid his gloves in the center of the octagon, and retired.

This is the full story behind that night. A kid from Simpsonville, South Carolina who earned his nickname at fifteen, went 57-0 as a kickboxer, came up through his dad's karate school, fought Tyron Woodley twice for the welterweight title, and spent fourteen years as the nicest man in the UFC. Then the hard years, the decision to walk away, and the kick that made Joe Rogan cry on the broadcast.
