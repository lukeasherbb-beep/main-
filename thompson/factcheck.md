## Confirmed
- Nickname at 15 against a 26-year-old with a 20-0 record (Sportskeeda; UFC.com gives the age as early-to-mid twenties). 1998 fits his birth date.
- Born Feb 11, 1983. Ray Thompson, former kickboxer, runs Upstate Karate in Simpsonville, SC; Stephen leads the kids' program.
- 57-0 combined kickboxing (37 amateur + 20 pro), 16 amateur titles, 2005 WAKO world title, #1 in Chuck Norris's World Combat League (2006).
- 5-0 before the UFC; UFC 143 debut KO (Feb 4, 2012); seven-fight UFC win streak with Whittaker R1 TKO, Ellenberger spinning kick KO (TUF 21 Finale, July 12, 2015), Hendricks R1 TKO, MacDonald decision.
- UFC 205, Nov 12, 2016, MSG, same card as McGregor vs Alvarez; Woodley dropped him in Round 4 (most sources say twice); majority draw 47-47, 47-47, 48-47 Woodley. UFC 209 rematch, March 2017, Woodley by majority decision.
- Beat Masvidal; losses to Till, Pettis (first KO loss, 2019), Burns, Belal Muhammad, Rakhmonov (rear-naked choke, UFC 296, Dec 16, 2023), Buckley (KO, UFC 307, Oct 5, 2024), Bonfim.
- Daughter about five months old (one source says four), "Wonderdad", told Helwani "I don't see the title anymore", body/damage concerns at 43.
- Radtke was 12-5 (5-2 UFC). He took Thompson down inside the first minute; Radtke won Round 1 on Sherdog's card.
- Spinning kick KO at 2:54 of Round 3, Marc Goddard stopped it. Gloves left in the center. Said "I told myself I wasn't going to cry." Rogan cried on the broadcast. Forbes headline: "the greatest retirement fight victory in UFC history."
- Final record 18-9-1; 13-9-1 in the UFC (12-9-1 going in).
- Helwani gave him an "NMF" belt (reported as 2019).
