Josh Hokit lost his heavyweight championship fight against Ciryl Gane at UFC 334 by a single round on all three scorecards, and before Bruce Buffer had even finished reading the decision, Jon Jones was filming himself telling Hokit to put his hand down. What followed was one of the ugliest online feuds in recent UFC history.

This is the full story: the middle finger in Bruce Buffer's face, the eye poke, Gane's record-breaking 237 significant strikes, Jones' "same coaches, different results" post, the war of words that followed on X, and why Alex Pereira, of all people, thinks Hokit was robbed. Plus Dana White, Conor McGregor and Mike Winkeljohn on where Hokit goes from here.

Did Hokit get robbed? And if Jones came out of retirement, who wins? Let me know in the comments.
