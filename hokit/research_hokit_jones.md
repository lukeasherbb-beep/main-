# Research: Josh Hokit vs Jon Jones beef (UFC 334 fallout)

Part 1 of SCRIPT_PROMPT.md. Compiled Nov 17, 2026 from published reports (links at the bottom). Anything that could not be confirmed is marked [UNVERIFIED]. Many of the posts are on X and some have been deleted; quotes below are as printed by the outlets.

## Key facts
- **UFC 334**, Saturday **Nov 14, 2026**, Madison Square Garden, New York. Main event: Ciryl Gane (c) def. Josh Hokit, unanimous decision, **48-47, 48-47, 48-47** (three rounds to two on every card). Co-main: Kayla Harrison def. Amanda Nunes, R4 rear-naked choke. (CBS Sports, MMA Junkie)
- Judges had it **2-2 going into the fifth round** (Dana White, Cageside Press).
- Dana White scored it **4-1 Gane** (49-46). (MMA Junkie, Cageside Press, BoxingNews)
- **Records after the fight:** Hokit 10-1 MMA, 4-1 UFC (first pro loss; he was 10-0). Gane 15-2 MMA, 12-2 UFC. (MMA Junkie)
- Both fighters got a **$100,000 Fight of the Night bonus**. (MMA Sucka, citing official UFC stats)

## Fight stats
- **Gane's 237 significant strikes** set a new single-fight UFC heavyweight record, passing the mark previously held by **Daniel Cormier**. With Hokit's 146, the pair also set the record for **most combined significant strikes in a UFC heavyweight fight**. (Yahoo Sports live blog)
- Hokit landed **146 of 283 significant strikes (51%)**, **3 takedowns**, **4:37 control time**. (MMA Sucka, official UFC stats)
- Round 3: Hokit out-struck Gane **71-37 in total strikes**; Gane's corner told him to rest. (Yahoo Sports)
- Rounds 4-5: Gane stopped **all 11** of Hokit's takedown attempts and out-struck him **158-84 in total strikes** over the final 10 minutes. (Yahoo Sports)
- Round 1: Hokit says he was poked in the eye early; referee **Marc Goddard** did not take a point or issue a warning. Late in the round Hokit landed a big single-leg slam. (Yahoo play-by-play, MMA Sucka, SPORTbible)
- Hokit flipped off Gane twice at the end of Round 3. (Yahoo play-by-play; MMA Sucka)
- Dana White: "even if you look at the significant strikes, they were double in every round (for Gane)." (Cageside Press)

## Josh Hokit background
- 29 years old. Former walk-on running back at **Fresno State**, also an All-American wrestler there; signed by the **San Francisco 49ers** as an undrafted player in 2020, never played a regular-season NFL game. (Wikipedia, EssentiallySports) [the UFC bio's "two years in the NFL" conflicts; avoid]
- Dana White's Contender Series win **Aug 19, 2025** (TKO R2, Guilherme Uriel) → UFC contract. UFC debut **Nov 8, 2025** (TKO of Max Gimenis, 0:56 R1). (Wikipedia, web search)
- Went **4-0 in seven months**, three by second-round TKO, including a Fight of the Year candidate with **Curtis Blaydes (April)** and a TKO of **Derrick Lewis at UFC Freedom 250, the White House card, in June**. (theScore, MMA Mania)
- Debut to Madison Square Garden title fight in about **12 months** (CBS Sports).
- Trains at **Jackson Wink MMA**, Albuquerque, coached by **Mike Winkeljohn** and **Greg Jackson**. (CBS Sports, SPORTbible)
- Known for a WWE-style persona and trash talk (theScore). Released a diss track ahead of UFC 334 ("MSG Freestyle", as "J-Word", per Agent MMA) aimed at Gane, Pereira, Topuria, Jones and others. Wore goggles at the press conference to mock Gane's eye-poke history. (MMA Sucka)

## Jon Jones context
- Former two-division UFC champion, **39**. Submitted Gane in round 1 at **UFC 285 (March 2023)** to win the heavyweight title. Retired; heavyweight belt left vacant. (MMA Junkie, LowKick)
- Youngest UFC champion ever at 23 (2011). (CBS Sports)
- Trained for years at Jackson Wink. **Winkeljohn barred Jones from his gym in 2021** after an arrest (misdemeanor battery, domestic violence, felony injuring/tampering with a vehicle), ending a 12-year relationship. Jones later called Winkeljohn a "miserable bastard." (CBS Sports)
- Mentors Olympic gold medalist **Gable Steveson**, who lost by **12-second KO to Sean Sharaf at UFC 331** (September 2026). (LowKick, BoxingNews)

## Open debates
1. **Did Hokit get robbed?** For Hokit: relentless pace, three takedowns, 4:37 control, won round 3 71-37 in total strikes, Pereira and many fans scored it for him. For Gane: 237 significant strikes (heavyweight record), double Hokit's in every round per Dana White, stuffed all 11 takedowns late, won the last two rounds clearly; Dana had it 4-1; media scorecards mostly 48-47 Gane.
2. **Does Hokit get an immediate rematch?** He asked; Dana: "Sure yeah, I'd do it again" [secondary report]. Competition: Alexander Volkov, Tom Aspinall if healthy, Pereira vs Pavlovich winner (UFC 335, Dec 12, Las Vegas).
3. **Jones vs Hokit: would Jones come out of retirement for it, and who wins?** Jones claims he dominated in sparring; Hokit says a 2026 Gane would beat Jones. No talks reported.

## Status (Nov 17)
- Jones says he's done addressing Hokit "until you get to a certain level", but kept posting afterwards. Some posts deleted.
- No rematch booked. Pereira fights Pavlovich at UFC 335 on Dec 12; Dana says a Pereira vs Hokit fight depends on that result.
