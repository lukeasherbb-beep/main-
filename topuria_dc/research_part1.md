## 1. Ilia Topuria: the domestic abuse allegations (Dec 2025 – Feb 2026, resolved)
**Important: there are no NEW allegations in November 2026.** The allegations date from December 2025 and the case was settled in February 2026. This week's news is an interview with his ex-wife that does not repeat the allegations.

Timeline
- **Aug–Sept 2025:** first reports that Topuria and his wife Giorgina Uzcategui (Badell) had split (outlets differ on the month). (EssentiallySports, Nov 16, 2026)
- **Oct 2025:** Topuria reportedly filed for civil divorce in Spain. (EssentiallySports)
- **Nov 2025:** Topuria steps away from defending the lightweight title; out of UFC 324 (Jan 24, 2026, where Gaethje vs Pimblett headlined for the interim belt). (Al Jazeera, Dec 16, 2025)
- **Dec 2025:** Uzcategui files a complaint accusing him of domestic and gender violence. Topuria firmly denies it. (EssentiallySports)
- **Dec 15, 2025, Topuria's Instagram statement** (quoted by Al Jazeera, Dec 16, 2025):
  - "In recent months, I have been subjected to severe and unacceptable pressure, including threats to disseminate false allegations of domestic abuse unless financial demands were met. These allegations are entirely unfounded."
  - "The truth is not a matter of opinion – it is a matter of evidence. All relevant evidence has been carefully preserved and documented, including audio recordings, written communications, witness statements, and video material."
  - Says the evidence went to the judicial authorities "to pursue legal action for attempted extortion, falsification of evidence, misappropriation of funds and personal property, and multiple threats."
- **Early Jan 2026:** Topuria calls a report that he was summoned to testify in a domestic violence case "fake news", saying the summons concerned his daughter and plans to take her out of Spain. (Bloody Elbow headline, Jan 3, 2026; search summary) [UNVERIFIED wording, page blocked]
- **Jan 7, 2026:** court rejects Uzcategui's request to move to Miami with their daughter. (Championship Rounds on X; Superlutas) [secondary]
- **Feb 5–6, 2026:** out-of-court settlement. Per ABC (Madrid), via MMA Mania (Feb 6, 2026): it "withdraws a complaint of domestic abuse"; she accepted "the same terms that Topuria had offered her from the beginning"; Topuria keeps the $3.5M Madrid home and gym; the daughter stays in Spain with Uzcategui, and Topuria gets a "generous visitation schedule." Marca / Irati Prat (via ClutchPoints/Yahoo, Feb 5, 2026): settlement signed on the terms Topuria originally offered.
- **June 14, 2026:** Topuria returns at UFC Freedom 250 (White House) and loses to Justin Gaethje, his first career loss; afterwards he accuses Gaethje of glove tampering, which Gaethje and the commission rejected. (Newsweek, MMA Sucka, June 2026)
