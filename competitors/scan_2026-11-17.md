## Packaging
- Titles: 2-3 stories stacked, caps on verbs, names first, "BREAKING!" for news under ~24 h.
- Thumbnails: collages of 3-4 faces, red arrows and circled insets, a quote caption in caps (often censored profanity). Fight Era thumbnails are clean single-subject documentary style (closer to doc channels than to these).
- Descriptions: chapter timestamps (MMA Talk, MMA Zone), source links (Full Mount). Sponsors on MMA Zone (Kalshi, MyBookie, Morgan & Morgan).
- Publish times: Full Mount ~18:00-19:00 UTC, MMA Talk ~14:00-15:00 UTC, MMA Zone ~19:00-21:00 UTC, plus late-night drops after events.
